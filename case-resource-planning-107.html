Case 02 · Resource & SLA Governance

Resource planning for a 107-person operation

Rebuilding capacity and resource planning for a 107-member team spanning US and international time zones, lifting utilisation 18% while cutting turnaround 40% and holding SLA adherence at 91%.

The challenge

Large-scale initiatives were being coordinated across US and international teams, with a 107-member group whose capacity was not planned against demand. Utilisation was uneven and turnaround times were long.

Commitments to internal and external stakeholders were not tracked in a way that showed when an SLA was about to be missed.

The approach

01

Mapped capacity to demand

Built resource planning across the full 107-member team so work was allocated against real availability rather than assumption.

02

Coordinated across time zones

Ran the initiatives across US and international teams with hand-offs planned around the working day rather than against it.

03

Put SLA tracking in place

Made SLA commitments visible on a cadence so slippage was caught early. Adherence held at 91% while turnaround fell 40%.

Takeaway

Utilisation is a planning problem before it is a people problem. Map capacity to demand honestly and the turnaround numbers follow.
