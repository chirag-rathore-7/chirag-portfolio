How I run delivery

Governance that people actually act on

Most delivery risk is visible weeks early if the plan and the RAID log are honest. The job is making the cadence tight enough that people act on what it shows — before it becomes an escalation.

01

Plan to the dependency, not the task

A WBS with the dependency map on top of it, so the critical path is visible and slippage surfaces before it is a crisis rather than after.

02

RAID is a living document

Risks and issues logged, owned and dated, reviewed on a fixed weekly cadence so escalation happens on time — not once the milestone is already gone.

03

One liaison, one source of truth

A single point between client and delivery teams, with scope, specs and UAT readiness in shared artefacts everyone plans against.
