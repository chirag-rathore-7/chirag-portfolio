Case 03 · Process Improvement

Eight improvement projects, run to milestones

Bringing milestone visibility to eight parallel client-focused improvement projects, optimising the underlying workflows and revamping onboarding. Cycle time down two days, onboarding productivity up 31%.

The challenge

Eight client-focused improvement projects were running in parallel without a shared milestone view, so it was hard to tell which were on track and where attention should go.

Onboarding was slow, holding back how quickly new team members reached productivity.

The approach

01

Put all eight on milestones

Introduced milestone tracking across every project so progress and slippage were comparable at a glance.

02

Optimised the workflows

Reworked the underlying process steps, taking two days out of the cycle time.

03

Revamped onboarding

Rebuilt the onboarding path, driving a 31% improvement in productivity for people coming into the team.

Takeaway

Parallel projects need one comparable view of progress. Once milestones are shared, it is obvious where a day of attention is worth most.
