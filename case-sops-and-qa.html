Case 04 · Standardisation & Quality

Standardising delivery with SOPs and QA

Turning undocumented process into SOPs and user guides, then adding quality-assurance checks that reduced errors by 14%.

Company
Better.com
Role
Senior Team Lead
Period
Jan 2020 – May 2021
Focus
SOPs · Documentation · QA
−14%
Reduction in errors
SOPs
Written across the core workflows

The challenge

Workflows varied between people and much of the process was undocumented, which showed up as quality variance and rework.

There was no consistent checkpoint to catch errors before they reached the next step.

The approach

01

Documented the process

Created SOPs, user guides and reference documentation so the same workflow was followed the same way every time.

02

Added QA checks

Built quality-assurance checks into the process, reducing errors by 14%.

Takeaway

Standardisation is not bureaucracy when it is written from how the best people already work. The QA check is what keeps it honest.
